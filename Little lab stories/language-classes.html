FAQ

Questions, Answered

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp — we answer personally.

Do I need to know some English to start?

No. Indonesian and Russian classes start from zero, and English classes are available from beginner level.

How long is a session, and how many will I need?

Sessions run 45–60 minutes. Most learners take one or two per week; a full CEFR level usually takes 20–30 sessions, depending on your starting point.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Let us know at least 12 hours before the session and we will move it, free of charge.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged on WhatsApp — bank transfer or e-wallet, per package. Everything is confirmed before your first class.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A certificate of completion is issued on request after you finish a level.

Do you teach children?

Yes. Young learner classes (ages 6–12) use stories, pictures and play, 45 minutes per session.

What if I live in another time zone?

Classes are scheduled in WIB (GMT+7). Evening slots work well for Europe, and early mornings for the Americas.

Can my company book a class?

Yes. Send us the number of learners and their goals, and we will propose a programme.

Ready to Start Learning?

Tell us your language and your goal. We will suggest the right class — and you can start this week.
